About these numbers

Reading the map

Each shape is one of BC's 93 provincial electoral districts, coloured by the party whose candidate is ahead there. A muted colour means the lead is under 5 percentage points. Grey means no votes have been reported yet.

Map area is not population: the large northern and interior districts fill most of the provincial map, while Metro Vancouver and Greater Victoria hold most of the seats on a small share of the land.

Where the count stands

These are Elections BC's unofficial results from the initial count, which covers ballots cast at advance voting and on Final Voting Day, and vote-by-mail ballots returned before advance voting ended. Mail-in and absentee ballots not counted tonight are added in the final count, planned for November 6 to 10, so close races can still change.

This page picks up new numbers automatically. A script checks Elections BC's results every few minutes, and the page looks for an update every 2 minutes while it is open.

Data checks

Every district on Elections BC's page must match one of the 93 districts on the map, and each district's candidate votes must add up to its published total. Anything that fails is listed here and shown exactly as published.
